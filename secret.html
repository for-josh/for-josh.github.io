What My Heart Really Wanted...

Kung lahi ra jd ang tanan.

Josh,

Kabalo ka… sometimes it scares me how often you cross my mind. Not in loud ways… but in quiet moments—kanang hilom na kaayo ang kalibutan, pero ikaw gihapon ang mugawas sa akong hunahuna.

I keep asking myself… What if things were different?

What if wala kay uyab… What if you weren’t straight… What if the world wasn’t built in a way that made something so real feel so wrong?

Maybe… just maybe… there could have been an “us.”

And that “maybe”… it hurts more than a “no.”

Because the truth is, if there was even the smallest chance, even the tiniest opening… I would have taken it without thinking twice.

I would have chosen you. Not just once… but every single day. Even on the days you didn’t choose me back.

Dili lang jud ni siya basta crush. What I felt for you… it stayed. It grew. It became something I couldn’t just laugh off or forget overnight.

It was the kind of feeling that quietly ruins you… kanang kabalo ka nga lisod… kabalo ka nga dili pwede… pero imong heart, dili man maminaw.

If things were different… I wouldn’t have loved you in silence.

I would have loved you out loud—without fear, without hesitation, without having to hide pieces of myself.

I would have cheered for you without holding back… looked at you without pretending I didn’t feel anything… talked to you without overthinking every word.

I would have been there—not just someone in the background, not just another face in the crowd… but someone you could actually hold onto.

Ganahan unta ko makaila pa nimo pag ayo… Not just the Joshua that everyone sees, but the Joshua behind your smiles—the Joshua I was quietly trying to understand.

And maybe… I would have let you see me too. Not the version that hides, but the one who cared this deeply… this genuinely.

Pero dili man gud tanan ginahatag sa ato.

Not all feelings get returned. Not all stories are meant to happen… no matter how much they make sense in your head.

And that’s what breaks me the most.

Because I know… if things were different… if timing was kinder… if the world wasn’t this complicated…

I would have loved you the way you deserve.

Softly. Patiently. Without conditions. Without fear of losing you before I even had you.

But instead… I’m left here learning how to let go of someone who was never really mine.

And that kind of pain… kanang “almost” lang… kanang you were so close, but never really there…

That’s the kind that doesn’t fade easily.

Because you weren’t just someone I liked.

You were someone I was ready to love completely… if only I was given the chance.

Goodbye, Josh 🤍
